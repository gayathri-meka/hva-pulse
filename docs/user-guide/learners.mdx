**Learners** is the master list of everyone in the programme — their identity, cohort, status, and who supports them.

## What's on each learner

- Name, email, phone, and category
- **Cohort** (financial year) and **batch**
- **Status** — e.g. Ongoing, Placed, Dropout, Discontinued
- **LF** (Learning Facilitator) and mentors assigned to them
- **Track** and join date

## Finding learners

Use the filters at the top to narrow by **cohort year** or view **all cohorts**. The table supports searching, sorting, per-column filters, and CSV export.

<Note>
The roster comes from the **Learner Roster** Google Sheet and is kept in sync. If a learner looks wrong or missing, check the sheet first — Pulse mirrors it. Syncing is done from **Settings → Sheets**.
</Note>
