**Learning** is where the team keeps learners healthy and on track. It brings together attendance, assessment scores, and course engagement, and lets you open a **case** when a learner needs support.

## What you'll find

- **Health signals** — attendance, assignment and quiz scores, and coding-task progress, pulled from Google Sheets and the SensAI learning platform.
- **Cases** — a structured way to help a struggling learner.
- **Settings** — configure metrics, data sources, and case categories (admins).

## How a case works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Flag">
    A learner is flagged when a signal looks off (low attendance, dropping scores).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Intervene">
    The team records the root cause and the action they're taking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor">
    Add update notes over time and, if needed, push the review date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolve">
    Close the case once the learner is back on track (or note the outcome).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Monitoring updates can be edited by any staff member; deleting an update is admin-only.
</Note>
