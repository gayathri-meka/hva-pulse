**Placements** is where the team runs hiring — the companies you work with, the roles they're hiring for, and each learner's progress through applications.

## The building blocks

- **Companies** — hiring partners (drag to reorder how they're shown).
- **Roles** — open positions at each company, with a job description.
- **Applications** — a learner applying to a role, moving through the pipeline.

## Application pipeline

A learner's application moves through these stages:

`applied → shortlisted → hired`

with two ways it can drop off:

- **Not shortlisted** — the company didn't pick them for an interview.
- **Rejected** — not selected after the interview.

When a learner is marked **not shortlisted** or **rejected**, the team adds a short reason, which the learner sees on their end.

## Also here

- **Matching** — line up learners against open roles.
- **Job Outreach** — pull in fresh job opportunities to source new roles.

## What learners see

Learners have their own portal showing roles they can apply to, their application status, and a place to manage their profile and résumé.
