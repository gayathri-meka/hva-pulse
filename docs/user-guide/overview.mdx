**Pulse** is HyperVerge Academy's internal operating system. It's where the team runs the whole learner journey in one place — from a candidate applying, through the programme, to a placement and beyond.

## Signing in

1. Go to Pulse and click **Sign in with Google**.
2. Use the Google account your admin added for you.

<Note>
Only accounts added to Pulse can sign in. If you can't get in, ask an admin to add your Google email under **Settings → Users**.
</Note>

## Who sees what

| You are | You get |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Everything, plus user management, data sources, and delete actions. |
| **Staff** | The full app — dashboard, learners, admissions, learning, placements, alumni. |
| **Learner** | A personal portal for placements — roles, profile, and résumé. |
| **Applicant** | The public onboarding flow — interest form, challenge, and their result. |
