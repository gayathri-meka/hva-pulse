## Alumni

**Alumni** tracks learners after they're placed — where they landed and how the cohort did overall.

- Per-alumnus: **company**, **role**, and **salary**.
- **Cohort analytics** — placement rates and outcomes by cohort year.

<Note>
For the current cohort, a learner's company, role, and salary fill in automatically when the team marks their application as **hired** in Placements. Older cohorts come from the alumni Google Sheet.
</Note>

## Ask Pulse

**Ask Pulse** lets you ask questions about Pulse's data in plain English, instead of digging through tables.

Ask things like:

- "How many learners are placed in the 2025-26 cohort?"
- "Which companies hired the most this year?"
- "Show me learners flagged in Learning with low attendance."

Pulse reads its own data to answer, so you get numbers grounded in what's actually in the system.
