Admissions covers everyone before they become a learner: people who applied, took the 14-day challenge, and are waiting on a decision.

## The candidate journey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Interest form">
    An applicant fills in the interest form (college, education, work, background).
  </Step>
  <Step title="14-day challenge">
    They join the challenge on SensAI and work through daily tasks. Pulse shows their progress day by day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    The team reviews each candidate and decides whether they move forward to an interview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Selection">
    Selected candidates are told they're through and to wait for interview scheduling.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The tabs

- **Prospects** — everyone who filled the interest form.
- **Website hits** — form submissions from the marketing site, matched to Pulse sign-ups.
- **Challenge** — challenge progress per candidate, with several views (day-by-day, pace, by-question, and **Review**).
- **Analytics** — trends for the admissions funnel (joined → started → completed).

## The Review tab

This is where the team decides who advances. For each candidate, Pulse shows a **system suggestion** (select / reject) based on a set of criteria, grouped into:

- **Need** — financial need signals (per-capita income, college, SES).
- **Work & Availability** — can they commit? (graduation timeline, current work).
- **Engagement** — how seriously they took the challenge (questions attempted, active days, consistency).

<Note>
The system's suggestion is only a recommendation. A team member always **verifies** before anything is final — and nothing reaches the candidate until it's verified.
</Note>

Open a candidate's **drawer** (the "Review" button) to see the full picture: every criterion, their raw intake answers, and their commitment / family-background answers.

<Warning>
Recording decisions is currently **locked** while the criteria are being finalised — the Select/Reject buttons are greyed out on purpose. They'll be switched on when the team is ready.
</Warning>
